The uncomfortable part

Why doesn't ranking on Google get you recommended by AI?

Because they're answering different questions. Google ranks pages against a query and lets you choose. An assistant picks one to three businesses and explains why, which means it isn't sorting — it's vouching. A system that vouches needs agreement between sources, not authority on one page, and that changes what wins.

What ranks a page on Google

The traditional signal set
  • Backlinks and domain authority
  • Keyword targeting and on-page relevance
  • Proximity to the searcher, for local results
  • Page speed and Core Web Vitals
  • Content depth on the specific page

What gets a business named by AI

Overlapping, but not the same
  • Agreement across sources — profile, site and directories saying identical things
  • Review volume and recency; picks average around 4.3 stars
  • Being mentioned on many pages an assistant already cites
  • Structured data a machine can read without inferring
  • Content that answers a question in a self-contained way
  • Presence on Bing, which ChatGPT's live lookups run on

The practical consequence catches contractors out constantly: a business can rank first in the map pack and never be named by an assistant, because the assistant found three different phone numbers for it across the web and quietly stopped trusting the record. Reported research puts the overlap between map pack winners and AI-recommended businesses at around 45%, which means roughly half the businesses winning on Google are losing on the surface their customers are increasingly using instead. There's also a reported pattern of businesses under roughly 150 reviews rarely being named at all — not a rule, but a strong enough signal to plan around.

The work itself

What does AI search optimization for contractors actually involve?

Six things, none of them exotic, and most of the first-order gains come from the least interesting ones. Anyone selling you a proprietary AI ranking system is selling something that doesn't exist — there is no ad product that buys placement inside an assistant's recommendations, which is exactly why the earned version compounds.

Step 01

Make every source agree

Name, address, phone, hours, services and service area identical across your website, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect and every directory carrying you. Assistants cross-reference these to verify facts, and disagreement reads as unreliability. This is the single highest-yield fix and it costs nothing but an afternoon of checking.

Step 02

Get onto Bing, not just Google

ChatGPT's live web lookups run on Bing's index, and Copilot does too. A contractor with an immaculate Google presence and no Bing Places listing is invisible to a large share of AI local search. Gemini, by contrast, is grounded in Google Maps, so profile corrections there can surface within days.

Step 03

Structured data on every page

LocalBusiness and Service markup, FAQ markup that matches what's visible, and speakable selectors. This is the difference between a machine reading your hours and a machine guessing at them. It's cheap, invisible to customers, and missing from the overwhelming majority of contractor sites.

Step 04

Answer-shaped content

Pages that answer one real question completely, with dated specifics and named sources rather than adjectives. Assistants quote self-contained passages, so a paragraph that only makes sense after reading the three above it doesn't get used. Content untouched for more than six months is reported to be at a growing disadvantage in citation pools.

Step 05

Review volume and recency

The signal that most reliably separates named businesses from invisible ones. Reported picks average around 4.3 stars, and businesses under roughly 150 reviews rarely surface. A steady trickle after every completed job builds this; occasional campaigns don't, and bursts look wrong to both Google and the assistants.

Step 06

Check robots.txt actually lets them in

GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended all read robots.txt. A great many sites block them by default through a setting nobody revisited, which removes the business from AI answers entirely while everything else looks fine. Thirty seconds to check.

On timing: new or refreshed content is reported to begin earning AI citations within three to five days, but building consistent visibility across assistants generally takes two to four months of sustained work — similar to traditional search timelines. The early gains come from the free fixes above; the compounding gains come from reviews and third-party mentions built over time. They aren't one thing

Do ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity pick businesses the same way?

No, and treating "AI search" as a single channel is the most common mistake made here. They draw on different indexes and update on different timescales, which means the same fix can show up in one within days and take weeks to appear in another. Knowing which is which tells you what to do first.

Assistant

ChatGPT

Its live web lookups run on Bing's index, cross-referenced against your website, your Google Business Profile and high-traffic consumer platforms. This is why a contractor with a perfect Google presence and no Bing Places listing can be invisible to it. Changes generally take weeks to filter through, since they depend on crawled pages and third-party sources rather than a single live feed.

Assistant

Gemini

Grounded in Google Maps, which makes it the fastest to reflect work you do. Profile corrections can surface within days once Google itself has picked them up. If you're going to fix one thing and want to see whether it moved anything, fix the Google Business Profile and check here first — it's the shortest feedback loop available.

Assistant

Perplexity

Citation-first by design, showing its sources openly. That makes it the most useful diagnostic tool of the three, whatever its share of actual traffic — you can see precisely which pages built an answer, which tells you where a competitor is being mentioned and you aren't. Use it to find out what to fix, not just whether you appear.

Also counts

Google AI Overviews

Not an assistant, but the same discipline and by far the largest volume. Reported figures put AI Overviews triggering on roughly 40% of local-intent queries. Appearing inside one is now a ranking outcome in its own right, and it sits above the organic results your existing SEO work is aimed at.

The common thread

They all cross-reference

Whatever the index underneath, every one of them checks your claims against other sources before repeating them. That's why consistency beats cleverness here. There is no phrasing trick that compensates for a profile and a website disagreeing about your hours.

What we won't claim

These systems don't publish their selection criteria, and anyone telling you they know the algorithm is guessing with confidence. What's above is drawn from published research and observed behaviour, and it will change. We'd rather tell you that than sell certainty that doesn't exist.

Straight answers

AI search optimization questions

What is AI search optimization for contractors?
It is the work of making your business the one an AI assistant names when somebody asks it for a contractor in your area. It overlaps heavily with ordinary search optimisation but rewards different things: agreement between your website, your Google Business Profile and every directory carrying you, structured data a machine can read without guessing, review volume and recency, presence on Bing as well as Google, and content that answers one question completely rather than ranking for a keyword. You will see it called AEO or GEO as well, and for a local business those are the same job.
Does ranking on Google mean AI will recommend me?
No, and that assumption costs contractors visibility every day. Reported 2026 research found only about 45 percent overlap between businesses winning the Google map pack and businesses AI assistants recommend, and just 12 percent of ChatGPT citations matching URLs on Google's first page for the same query. Google ranks pages and lets you choose; an assistant names one to three businesses and explains why, which means it is vouching rather than sorting. Vouching requires agreement between sources, so inconsistent information hurts far more than it does in traditional search.
How do I check whether ChatGPT recommends my business?
Ask it, in a logged-out or private window so it is not simply reflecting your own history back at you. Use non-branded prompts phrased the way a customer would speak, combining your service, your city and a specific need, and record whether you were named. Run the same prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, because they draw on different sources, and repeat them across several days since answers vary between sessions. The Prompt Probe on this page generates twelve such prompts for your trade and city and keeps score for you.
How long does AI search optimization take to work?
New or refreshed content is reported to begin earning AI citations within three to five days, which is considerably faster than traditional search. Building consistent visibility, meaning being named reliably across several assistants, typically takes two to four months of sustained work on listings, reviews and content. Gemini is grounded in Google Maps so profile corrections there can surface within days, while ChatGPT and Perplexity depend on crawled pages and third-party sources and generally take weeks to reflect changes.
Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT's recommendations?
No. There is currently no advertising product that places a business inside an assistant's organic recommendations, which is precisely why optimising for it early is worth doing. Visibility here is earned rather than bought, so a contractor who does the unglamorous work now builds a referral channel that a better-funded competitor cannot simply outspend them for. That may not remain true indefinitely, but it holds today.
Do reviews matter more than backlinks for AI visibility?
For local businesses, reported evidence suggests yes. Consistent business data and review signals appear to outweigh a classic backlink profile when an assistant decides who to name. Reported analysis puts the average rating of businesses ChatGPT picks at around 4.3 stars, with businesses under roughly 150 reviews rarely being named at all. Treat that as a strong directional signal rather than a threshold, but it does mean review velocity is usually a better investment than link building for this specific goal.
Could my website be blocking AI crawlers by accident?
Yes, and it is more common than most contractors expect. GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and others all read your robots.txt file, and a great many sites block them through a default setting or a plugin option nobody revisited. The effect is that your business disappears from AI answers entirely while every other metric looks completely normal, which makes it hard to diagnose and trivial to fix. It takes about thirty seconds to check.
Is this worth doing if the traffic volume is still small?
The volume is smaller than Google today and the quality is reportedly much higher, because a customer arriving on an assistant's recommendation has been vouched for rather than presented with ten options. Reported figures put ChatGPT referral conversion at around 15.9 percent against roughly 1.76 percent for Google organic. Combined with the fact that adoption reportedly moved from 6 percent to 45 percent of consumers in a single year, the argument is less about today's traffic than about being established before the channel is contested.
