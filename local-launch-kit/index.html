What is the Local Launch Kit?

It is a fixed-price contractor marketing package from Advertising For Contractors: $497 to set up, then $97 a month. The setup gets your business listed and verified, publishes one press release, makes three SEO fixes on your site, switches on review requests after every job and wires your website form to reply to new leads within seconds. The monthly fee keeps the listings, review requests and lead replies running. It does not buy leads. It builds the things that make you easier to find and easier to choose, and every call it produces comes straight to you.

We built it because the same conversation kept happening. A roofer or a remodeler with good work and a thin online footprint is paying a marketplace for shared leads, getting a handful of reviews a year because nobody remembers to ask, and losing evening form leads to whoever answers first. None of that needs a $2,000-a-month retainer to fix. It needs a short list of jobs done properly, once, and then kept running.

Local Launch Kit questions

What does the Local Launch Kit cost?
$497 once for the setup, paid at checkout. The $97 monthly plan starts 30 days later and renews monthly until you cancel, which you can do online at any time. There is no contract, no cancellation fee and no charge per lead. If you want more than the three SEO fixes in the kit, extra fixes are $100 each.
Will the kit guarantee me leads or a ranking?
No. Nobody can honestly promise a number of leads or a ranking position for a fixed fee, because both depend on your market, your competitors, your reviews and your service area. We guarantee the work and the dates: listings live, press release published, three fixes made, review requests and lead replies running. If we miss a date we promised, tell us and we credit that month's $97.
How is this different from buying leads on Angi or Thumbtack?
Marketplaces sell you a chance at a customer they found. Angi's own FAQ says a Market Match request goes to up to four pros, and Thumbtack charges for a lead whether or not you get hired. The kit spends your money on things you keep: accurate listings, a growing review count, fixes on your own website and fast replies to people who found you. Plenty of contractors run both for a while and lean on the marketplace less as their own leads grow.
I already have a website. Do I still need this?
Probably, because the kit is not a website. It is the work around your website that most contractors never get to: listings that match, a steady stream of review requests, a press release that gives you a citable mention, three fixes on pages you already have, and instant replies to form leads. If you think your site is beyond three fixes, call first and we will tell you honestly whether the kit is the right first step.
Which directories are included?
USABusinessSearch.com, our nationwide business directory, plus COBusinessList.com if you work in Colorado. You get a Verified listing and a Featured placement in your trade category, kept active while the $97 plan runs. Contractors outside Colorado get a fourth SEO fix on their website instead of the second directory.
Can I cancel the $97 plan?
Yes, any time, using the cancel link in your receipt email or by emailing us. No phone call and no fee. The Featured placements, review requests and automatic lead replies stop at the end of the month you paid for. The website fixes, the published press release and every review you earned stay yours, and your review link and QR card keep working because they point at your own Google profile.
