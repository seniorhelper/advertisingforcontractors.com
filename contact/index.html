What actually happens when you get in touch?

A conversation about your business, not a pitch about ours. We ask what an average job invoices at, what you close, where you work and what your slow season looks like. Without those we can't tell you anything useful, and with them we usually can inside twenty minutes.

You won't get a sequence. No automated emails, no "just checking in" every Tuesday, no calendar link that leads to a sales rep reading a script. One conversation with someone who does this work.

You'll get a real number. Not a range designed to get a second meeting. If your market needs $4,000 a month to win, we say $4,000. If $300 would do it, we say that too and we quote accordingly.

You might get told no. If your break-even cost per lead is too low for paid channels to clear, or your service area is already locked up by a client of ours, or the honest answer is to spend the money on a second truck — we'll say so. Losing that sale costs us less than a bad six months costs both of us.

Questions

Before you call

How quickly will someone get back to me?
Within one business day for email and form messages, and usually the same day. If you call during business hours, Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm Mountain Time, a person answers directly. Outside those hours leave a message and we call back first thing. Free audits are typically turned around in about one business day.
Will I get put into an email sequence?
No. There is no automated follow-up behind this form, no drip campaign and no mailing list. A person reads what you send and replies. If you do not want to go further, saying so once is enough and you will not hear from us again.
What should I have ready before I call?
Four things make the first conversation far more useful: what an average job invoices at, roughly what your gross margin is, what percentage of leads you close, and the cities you cover. Those four numbers let us work out your break-even cost per lead, which determines what can realistically work in your market. If you do not know them, the free plan builder on this site will walk you through it in about four minutes.
Do you work with contractors outside Colorado?
Yes. We are based in Denver but we work with contracting companies across the United States, Canada and internationally. What matters is whether we can realistically win in your market and whether the economics work for your business, not where you are located.
Is the free audit really free, and is there a catch?
It is genuinely free and there is no catch. We look at your website, your Google Business Profile, your reviews and the competitors currently outranking you, and you keep the findings whether you hire us or not. Plenty of contractors run the audit, act on it themselves and never become clients. That is a fine outcome.
What if I am not a contractor?
Our parent company, Eye To Ad Media, works across most other industries including dental, legal, med spa, real estate, restaurants and home services outside the trades. Get in touch here and we will route you, or look at the industries beyond contracting page for the relevant specialist pages.
