The trade

Why is marketing for painting contractors so expensive?

Because the barrier to entry is a ladder and a truck, so the auction is crowded with everybody from two-man crews to twenty-crew operations bidding on the same words. Painting carries the highest cost per click in home services, and prices rose 38% in two years. You cannot win that auction on budget. You win it by owning channels nobody can outbid you on.

Factor 01

The most crowded auction in the trades

At $13.74 a click you are paying more than plumbers, electricians and HVAC companies for cold traffic, on smaller tickets than any of them. Every dollar of that is a dollar you are not spending on the customers who already hired you once.

Factor 02

A two-and-a-half-times close rate gap

Ad leads close near 20%. Referrals close above 50%. No sales training closes that gap, because it is not about selling — it is the difference between a stranger with four quotes and a neighbour who was told to call you.

Factor 03

Your work sells itself, on camera

Painting is the most visually dramatic trade there is. Published data puts video at 30–60% lower cost per lead than static ads, with owner-filmed footage beating agency production by 25–40%. Your phone genuinely outperforms a crew.

Factor 04

Every customer expires and renews

Repaints come round every five to seven years, which means a fixed share of everyone you have ever worked for is due right now. No other trade has such a predictable second sale, and almost no painter has a system for collecting it.

Factor 05

Cabinets are a different business

Cabinet refinishing carries higher margin, thinner competition and a considered buyer rather than a price shopper. Run inside a general painting campaign it gets bid and written as though it were wall work, which is money left on the table twice.

Factor 06

The season decides the budget

Exterior demand is weather-bound, interiors carry the cold months, and the booking decision happens weeks before the work. Spending evenly across twelve months means overpaying in the quiet ones and running out in the ones that matter.

Timing

When should a painting contractor spend the money?

Not evenly. Painting demand is weather-shaped and the booking decision happens weeks before the work, which means the spend has to lead the season rather than follow it. Spread a budget flat across twelve months and you overpay in the quiet ones and run dry in the ones that pay for your year.

Late winter

Book the exterior season

Homeowners plan spring exteriors in February and March. Being visible before the weather turns is how the calendar fills; being visible in May is how you compete for what is left.

Spring & summer — peak

Defend the peak, do not discover it

Highest volume and highest competition simultaneously. Costs per lead climb in peak months, which is survivable if the season was booked in advance and brutal if it was not.

Autumn

Push interiors early

The switch from exterior to interior happens in the customer’s head before it happens in your schedule. Move the message in September, not when the crews go idle in November.

Winter

Cabinets, commercial and the list

Cabinet refinishing, occupied commercial work and reactivation emails are what keep crews paid through the cold months. All three are cheapest to market when everyone else has gone quiet.

This is the one genuinely time-sensitive thing on this page, and it is not a sales tactic — it is a calendar. Work booked for a season starts about eight to twelve weeks before the season does. If you are reading this and your next quarter is not full, that decision is being made right now whether you participate in it or not.

The work

What does painting contractor SEO and advertising involve?

The free and the owned first, because in painting they outperform paid by a distance. Profile and reviews, then the customer list, then the pages that separate your service lines. Paid comes once there is somewhere worth sending a $13.74 click.

Step 01 · Cheapest work you will get

Customer list & referral systems

Reactivation campaigns to everyone due a repaint, automatic review requests after every job, and a referral programme that runs as a process rather than a hope. Closes above 50% against roughly 20% on ads, and costs an email.

Lead generation systems Step 02 · Free and fastest

Google Business Profile & the map pack

Correct categories, every service listed including cabinet refinishing, genuine before-and-after photography rather than stock, accurate service area and a steady review habit. Movement usually shows in 30 to 90 days. Google's own Business Profile help centre documents it if you would rather self-serve.

Local SEO for contractors Step 03 · Separate the money

A page per service line

Interior, exterior, cabinet refinishing and commercial are four different searches by four different buyers at four different price points. One services page cannot rank for or speak to all of them, and cabinets in particular deserve their own everything.

Where should a painting contractor get independent information?

Not from a marketing agency, on most of this. These are the primary sources we use ourselves and none of them pay us to be listed. On coatings, safety and lead paint especially, go to the standards body or the regulator rather than to anyone selling you something.

pdca.org

PCA — Painting Contractors Association

The trade association for painting contractors. Standards, business education, certification and industry advocacy. Membership is a credibility signal worth displaying if you hold it.

epa.gov

EPA — Lead-Safe Certification

RRP rule requirements for work on pre-1978 housing. Certification is a legal obligation on much residential repaint work and a genuine differentiator worth stating plainly on your site.

osha.gov

OSHA

Fall protection, respiratory protection and solvent handling. The obligations are not optional and a clean record is a real asset when bidding commercial work.

paint.org

American Coatings Association

Industry body for the coatings manufacturers. Technical material, regulatory tracking and product standards — a better citation than a manufacturer's marketing page.

sba.gov

U.S. Small Business Administration

Free counselling, financing guidance and local assistance offices. If your constraint is working capital or crew rather than leads, this is a better first call than any agency.

developers.google.com

Google Search Central

Google's own documentation on how search works and what structured data does. The antidote to most of the SEO folklore you will be told by people selling it.

Straight answers

Painting contractor marketing questions

How much should a painting contractor spend on marketing?
Published figures put painting contractors at roughly 3% to 10% of revenue, with the top-performing companies investing 7% to 12% across digital channels. In practice most of the painting companies we work with land between $1,500 and $5,000 a month. But the percentage is the least useful way to decide. Work backwards instead: the revenue you want, divided by average job value, divided by your close rate, multiplied by your cost per lead. That gives you a number you can defend, and it frequently comes out lower than the percentage rule suggests.
What is a good cost per lead for a painting contractor?
Google Ads leads for painters ran a median of about $174 in 2026 across 1,270+ tracked leads, up 38% from $126 in 2024. Local Services Ads came in around $48 — roughly a third of the search cost. Meta and Facebook leads generally run $45 to $80, and blended figures across channels sit near $138. Well-optimised search campaigns reach $67 to $128; broad campaigns pointed at a homepage instead of a service page routinely pay $343 or worse. The number that decides anything, though, is cost per booked job, not cost per lead.
Why do painters pay the highest cost per click in the trades?
Painting carries an average cost per click of about $13.74 in 2026 — higher than plumbing, HVAC or electrical. It is a crowded auction with a low barrier to entry: anybody with a ladder and a truck can bid on “painters near me”, and many do. The result is that painting contractors compete on price in the auction and then again on the estimate. That is exactly why the channels you own matter more in this trade than in most — you cannot be outbid on a customer who already has your number.
Why do my ad leads close so much worse than my referrals?
Because they are strangers and your referrals are not. Published 2026 painting benchmarks put ad-generated leads closing at roughly 20% while referral leads close above 50%. That is not a reflection on your sales ability — it is the difference between somebody comparing four quotes and somebody whose neighbour already vouched for you. The practical consequence is that a referral is worth more than double an ad lead to you, and most painting companies have no system for producing them on purpose.
Is it worth marketing to my past painting customers?
It is usually the highest-return thing a painting contractor can do, and almost nobody does it. Interior repaints commonly come round every five to seven years and exteriors on a similar cycle, which means a meaningful slice of everyone you have ever painted for is due right now. They already know your work, they close at referral-like rates, and reaching them costs an email rather than $174. The calculator on this page prices that list against what buying the same jobs would cost.
Are Local Services Ads worth it for painters?
For most painting contractors, yes, and especially below about $2,000 a month in spend. Painting LSA leads run near $48 against roughly $174 on standard search, you pay per lead rather than per click, and the listing sits above the regular ads. Two things most contractors miss: Google Guaranteed was renamed Google Verified in October 2025, so older guides use the wrong name, and junk leads can be disputed for a refund — most companies never file, which quietly inflates their real cost per lead.
Does video actually work for painting companies?
It is the one channel where painting has a structural advantage over every other trade, because the work is visually dramatic in a way a rebuilt condenser is not. Published 2026 data indicates video ads deliver 30% to 60% lower cost per lead than static images for painting contractors, and that owner-on-camera footage outperforms agency-produced content by 25% to 40% on cost per lead. Which means the phone in your pocket beats a production crew. Before-and-afters, shot badly, by you.
How long does SEO take for a painting contractor?
Google Business Profile and map pack movement usually shows in 30 to 90 days, and for painters that is where a disproportionate share of the value sits — reviews and photos do more work in this trade than long-form content does. Competitive organic terms take four to eight months. Interior, exterior, cabinet refinishing and commercial should each have their own page rather than sharing one services page, because they are different searches by different buyers at different price points.
Should interior, exterior and cabinet work be marketed separately?
Yes, and cabinet refinishing especially. It carries a materially higher margin than standard interior work, the search competition is thinner, and the buyer is making a considered decision rather than a price comparison. Running it inside a general painting campaign means bidding wall-painting money for cabinet work and speaking to a cabinet customer in wall-painting language. Separate page, separate ad group, separate photography.
Will AI search send painting customers my way?
Increasingly. Homeowners ask assistants what a repaint should cost, how long paint lasts and whether cabinets can be refinished rather than replaced — all before they look for anyone to do the work. Research published in 2026 estimates only about 1.2% of local businesses surface in AI-generated answers, while visitors arriving from AI convert at roughly 14.2% against 2.8% from traditional search. Honest local cost content is the qualifying ticket, and it is the same content that filters your enquiries before you drive out.
